## Aims

- **[Cover real policies](https://codeberg.org/open-source-ecosystem/ai-policy/src/branch/main/README.md#aims)**: the actual spread, not an ideal
- **Surface the questions**, including unasked ones
- **Enable research**: policies studyable as data
- **Clearer than prose** about a policy's shape

**Coverage, questions and research pull towards<br />more structure, and so against easy adoption.**

[↳ README: aims](https://codeberg.org/open-source-ecosystem/ai-policy/src/branch/main/README.md#aims)

Note:

↓ Keeping both

- The design rules on the next slide serve these aims and are judged by them
- Cover real policies: a policy the format cannot express is a finding to act on, not an edge case to ignore
- Surface the questions: a project sees what there is to decide without having to decide all of it at once
- Enable research: what projects decide, why, what they leave open, and how that changes, in one vocabulary under version control
- Clearer than prose about the *shape*, not about the judgements: what counts as menial, understood or significant stays in words; structure that pretended otherwise would be false precision

## Keeping both

<!-- .slide: data-timing="0" -->

- **Presets** that list what they leave unaddressed
- **`undecided`** for what is discussed, not settled
- **`notes`** first, structure only once it recurs
- A [guide](https://codeberg.org/open-source-ecosystem/ai-policy/src/branch/main/writing-a-policy.md#working-it-out) for working the rest out

**Breadth lives in the schema;<br />a project meets only what is still open for it.**

[↳ Guide: if you already know where you stand](https://codeberg.org/open-source-ecosystem/ai-policy/src/branch/main/writing-a-policy.md#if-you-already-know-where-you-stand)

Note:

- Most projects do not need to design a policy from scratch: take a preset from `examples/` and stop there
- Each preset except Neutral ends with a "Not addressed by this preset" section: commented-out lines showing what else a project might settle
- The presets mirror ai-policy.dev's prose texts, plus Research Only (structured file only, no prose preset yet) and Neutral (which points `statement` at the ordinary contribution guide)
- The guide's order: settle the common ground, name the concerns, give each its narrowest lever, argue the stance last
